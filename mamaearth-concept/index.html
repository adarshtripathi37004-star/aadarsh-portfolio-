01 — The Story

Every festival begins with a diya, and a glow.

Long before filters and serums, India lit lamps, rubbed on turmeric and sandalwood, and called it a ritual. This season we bring that wisdom to a modern shelf: skincare that is kind to skin, kind to the planet, and honest about what's inside.

“Festivals are not for looking perfect. They are for feeling radiant.”
