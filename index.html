About me

Analytics brain, brand instinct.

I'm Aadarsh Dhananjay Tripathi — a marketing graduate who started in finance and found my home where data meets storytelling. After a BBA in Finance in Pune, India, I moved to the UK to study an MSc in International Marketing Management with Marketing Analysis at the University of Leeds, because I wanted to understand not just what customers do, but why — and how to act on it.

Today I work as a Junior Marketing Analyst at the University of Leeds, where I analyse competitor campaigns, build reporting dashboards, and run institutional events end-to-end. Outside of that, I founded EveryBizAI — an AI-powered luxury brand-intelligence page where I own everything from editorial strategy to an automated content pipeline.

What ties it together is range: I'm as comfortable in R Studio building a clustering model as I am shaping a brand voice and a content calendar. I'm looking for a graduate role in marketing, marketing analytics or brand where both sides of that count.
